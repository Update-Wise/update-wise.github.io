# Frequently asked questions

## What is Update Wise?

It's a tool that analyzes your PC's hardware and recommends compatible upgrades based on how you use it and your budget.

## How much does it cost?

There's a free plan to understand your machine and paid plans with detailed analysis and a step-by-step guide. See the [pricing section](/en/#pricing).

## Which systems does it run on?

Windows, Linux, and macOS. Pick your platform on the [download page](/en/download).

## How does it detect my hardware?

The app reads technical information about your components (processor, memory, storage, graphics, and motherboard) directly from your machine.

## What happens with my data?

We only use the information needed to provide features and improve the service. See the [privacy policy](/en/privacy) for details.

## Do I need an account?

No. You can analyze your PC without signing up.

## Does it work offline?

Hardware detection works locally. Some features, like downloading compatibility catalogs, need a connection.

## Where do I download the app?

On the [download page](/en/download), which detects your system automatically.

## How do I contact support?

Through the [support page](/en/support). Before writing, it's worth checking this page and the [service status](/en/status).
