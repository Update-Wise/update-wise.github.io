# About Update Wise

Update Wise is a tool that helps you understand your PC and decide what to upgrade — no unnecessary jargon, no generic advice.

## What we do

We analyze your machine's hardware —processor, memory, motherboard, storage, and graphics— and combine it with how you use your PC and your budget to suggest compatible upgrades that make sense.

Our flow is always the same:

1. **We detect** your PC's components.
2. **We analyze** your setup and your needs.
3. **We improve** with prioritized recommendations and a clear guide.

## Why we exist

Upgrading a PC shouldn't require being an expert or buying the most expensive part. We believe that with the right information, anyone can make a good decision. That's why we favor clarity over jargon and compatibility over marketing.

## Open source and community

Update Wise is an open project. You can read the code, suggest improvements, and report issues in our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Update-Wise/Update-Wise-Main).
