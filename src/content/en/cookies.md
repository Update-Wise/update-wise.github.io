# Cookie Policy

At Update Wise, we use cookies and similar technologies so that our site works properly, to remember certain preferences, and to understand how the platform is used.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website can store on your device when you visit it. They make it possible to remember information during browsing and enable certain features.

## How do we use cookies?

Update Wise may use cookies to:

- Keep the basic operation of the site running.
- Remember preferences, such as the theme setting.
- Improve the browsing experience.
- Obtain aggregate information about site usage, where applicable.

## Essential cookies

Some cookies may be necessary to provide certain site features. These cookies are not used to personally identify you, and their purpose is related to the operation of the platform.

## Preference cookies

We may use local storage or other similar technologies to remember preferences selected by the user, such as light or dark mode.

These preferences are stored on the device and can be deleted from the browser settings.

## Third-party cookies

Update Wise may include external services that use their own storage technologies or cookies. When this happens, the handling of such information will also be subject to the respective providers' privacy policies.

## Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies from your browser settings. Please note that blocking certain cookies or storage technologies may affect some site features.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our features, technologies, or legal requirements change.

The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.

## Contact

If you have questions about the use of cookies at Update Wise, you can contact our team through the channels available on the website.
