# Welcome to the Update Wise blog

We're launching this blog to share what happens between releases: guides to get the most out of your PC, project news, and product decisions explained without the fluff.

## What we'll publish

- **Practical guides:** how to read your hardware report and what to upgrade first for the way you use your PC.
- **News:** new features, supported platforms, and recommendation-engine improvements.
- **Behind the scenes:** technical and design decisions, told by the team.

## The project in one sentence

Update Wise exists so upgrading your PC doesn't require being an expert: we detect your hardware, analyze it, and guide you to the upgrade that makes sense for you.

## Get involved

The project is open: the code lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/Update-Wise/Update-Wise-Main), where you can suggest ideas and report issues. And if you don't have it yet, download the app from the [download page](/en/download).
