# Privacy Policy

At Update Wise, we care about the privacy of the people who use our services. This policy explains what information we may collect, what we use it for, and what choices you have regarding it.

## Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Update Wise.

We may collect information such as:

- Technical device information needed to provide certain features.
- Information related to the use of the app and the website.
- Preferences you select within our services.
- Information you voluntarily provide when using certain features.

We do not collect personal information that is not necessary to provide or improve our services.

## Hardware information

When you use Update Wise's hardware analysis features, the app may access technical information about your device, such as:

- Processor.
- Graphics card.
- RAM.
- Storage.
- Motherboard.
- Operating system.

This information is used to analyze the characteristics of your machine and generate recommendations related to upgrades or improvements.

## What do we use the information for?

The information collected may be used to:

- Provide and maintain Update Wise's features.
- Generate hardware recommendations.
- Improve the operation and experience of our services.
- Detect and fix errors.
- Keep the platform secure.
- Comply with legal obligations where applicable.

We do not use the information collected to sell personal profiles to third parties.

## Storage and security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we process against unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or use.

No system connected to the Internet can guarantee absolute security. Therefore, while we strive to protect information appropriately, we cannot guarantee that any system is completely invulnerable.

## Third-party services

Update Wise may use services provided by third parties for certain platform functions, such as hosting, content delivery, technical analysis, or communications.

When these services process information, they may be subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

## Sharing information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information only when necessary to provide a service, protect the platform, comply with a legal obligation, or when the user has given consent where required.

## Information retention

We keep information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, provide our services, or comply with legal obligations.

The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and its purpose.

## Rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights related to your personal data, including requesting access, correction, deletion, or information about the processing of your data.

Requests can be made through the contact channels available on the website.

## Children's privacy

Update Wise is not designed to knowingly solicit personal information from children without the safeguards or authorizations that applicable laws may require.

If you believe a child has improperly provided personal information, you can contact us to request a review.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, information processing practices, or legal requirements change.

The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.

## Contact

If you have questions, requests, or concerns related to this Privacy Policy or to Update Wise's handling of information, you can contact our team through the channels available on the website.
