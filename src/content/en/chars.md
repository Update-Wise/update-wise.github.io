# UpdateWise Features

UpdateWise helps you understand your computer, evaluate potential upgrades, and make better-informed hardware decisions. Our goal is to make choosing hardware simpler, more transparent, and better suited to your needs.

## Now

These are the capabilities that define UpdateWise's current approach.

> ### Hardware Detection
>
> Identifies your computer's components so you can better understand your system and its specifications.
>
> ### Component Evaluation
>
> Analyzes hardware characteristics to help you understand how each component contributes to your system and evaluate potential upgrades.
>
> ### Recommendations Based on Your Needs
>
> Takes your budget, activities, and priorities into account to guide the evaluation toward options that make sense for you.
>
> ### Alternative Comparison
>
> Makes it easier to compare potential upgrades so you can weigh their advantages and limitations before investing.
>
> ### Focus on Compatibility
>
> Hardware compatibility is a fundamental part of the evaluation. An upgrade should make sense for your system as a whole, not just stand out because of its specifications.
>
> ### Information for Better Decisions
>
> UpdateWise aims to provide clear, understandable information so you can make your own decisions without relying solely on isolated numbers or brand popularity.

## Future

These are some of the areas we plan to develop to expand UpdateWise's capabilities.

> ### Hardware History
>
> Keeps records of hardware detections so you can track how your computer's configuration changes over time.
>
> ### System Monitoring
>
> Provides access to dynamic system metrics, such as temperatures and resource usage, to help you better understand your computer's behavior while running.
>
> ### More Accurate Evaluations
>
> Improves estimates through evaluation models and metrics that help compare hardware across different usage scenarios.
>
> ### More Operating Systems
>
> Expands compatibility with different operating systems so more people can use UpdateWise on their computers.
>
> ### Pricing and Availability Information
>
> Integrates market information to make it easier to find components, compare prices, and discover alternatives available in your region.
>
> ### More Comparison Tools
>
> Develops new ways to explore configurations, evaluate potential upgrades, and understand the advantages and limitations of each alternative.

---

**Our philosophy:** help you choose with information, not tell you what to buy. We want UpdateWise to evolve with you and help you get more out of your computer.
