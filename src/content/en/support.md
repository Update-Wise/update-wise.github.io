# Support and contact

## Contact

Have a question, suggestion, or need some help? We'd love to hear from you. Send us an email at our [contact email](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/?to=contact@updatewise.com.co&su=Inquiry%20-%20UpdateWise&body=Hello%2C%20UpdateWise%20team%3A&fs=1&tf=cm), and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

## Before writing

Most questions are answered in the [FAQ](/en/faq). If something isn't working, check the [service status](/en/status) first in case it's a known incident.

## Help channels

- **App issues:** open an issue on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Update-Wise/Update-Wise-Main/issues) with steps to reproduce it.
- **Ideas and improvements:** propose them in the [repository](https://github.com/Update-Wise/Update-Wise-Main).
- **Privacy and data:** see the [privacy policy](/en/privacy) for your rights and options.
- **Support Email:** send an email to our [support email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?view=cm&to=support@updatewise.com.co&su=Asunto&body=Hola%2C%20buenos%20d%C3%ADas.)

## How to report a bug

To help you faster, please include:

1. Your operating system and its version.
2. The Update Wise version you're using.
3. What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
4. Steps to reproduce the problem.

## Response times

We're a small team and answer in order of arrival. Security issues and bugs that block using the app take priority.
